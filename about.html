Who We Are

STAPHORD ELECTRICAL SOLUTIONS is a Tanzanian Partnership Business registered by BRELA on 03rd November 2026 under the Business Names Act (Cap 213).

Based in Mbeya CBD, Kalobe (near K.K.K.T Church Nzovwe), we specialize in electrical installation, solar power, and industrial electrical works. We are a young, energetic, and field-focused team working with experienced technicians to deliver quality work, safety, and timely completion.


Our Vision

To become the most trusted electrical solutions partner in Mbeya and Southern Highlands for homes, industries, and development projects.

Our Mission

To provide safe, affordable, and durable electrical installations that meet Tanzanian standards and exceed client expectations.
